# PrimeAura Academy Website

Create a modern, premium and minimal single-page website for "PrimeAura Academy", an online tuition academy.

Tagline: "Learn. Understand. Excel."

PrimeAura provides tuition for Classes 1–10, Plus One and Plus Two, following Kerala State Board and CBSE syllabuses. Online classes are available for students anywhere, while offline classes are available in Calicut only.

Target audience: Students and parents. The website should build trust and encourage parents to book a free demo class.

Design:

- Premium modern education brand

- Navy blue, gold, white with subtle pink accents

- Clean typography and generous whitespace

- Modern icons and subtle animations

- Fully responsive, especially mobile

- Avoid a generic school/tuition-centre look

Create these sections on one page:

1. Navbar with logo, Home, Classes, Mentors, About, Contact and "Book Free Demo" CTA

2. Hero section with:

   "Expert Guidance. Better Learning."

   "Live online tuition with expert mentors, personal attention and exam-focused learning."

   Classes 1–10 | Plus One | Plus Two

   Kerala State Board | CBSE

   Buttons: "Book Free Demo" and "WhatsApp Us"

3. Why Choose PrimeAura — Expert & Qualified Mentors, Live Interactive Classes, Personal Attention, Regular Tests, Doubt Clearing, Exam Preparation

4. Classes & Subjects — Mathematics, Science, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, Social Science, Computer Science and other subjects

5. Mentors section with editable placeholder cards; do not invent qualifications or achievements

6. Online Classes vs Offline Classes — Online available anywhere; Offline available in Calicut only

7. Strong Free Demo CTA

8. Contact section with:

   96057 66178

   73563 47117

9. Simple footer

Make the website conversion-focused with repeated "Book Free Demo" and "WhatsApp Us" buttons.

Do not add login, signup, payment, dashboard or unnecessary complex features.
